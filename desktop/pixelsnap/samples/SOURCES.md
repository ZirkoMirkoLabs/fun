# Sample sources

Downloaded 2026-10-28. No Etsy sources. Sample media is separately licensed from the MIT application.

There are ten input cases: six unchanged downloaded AI images, two deliberately degraded variants of those images, and two synthetic enlargements of frames from one downloaded genuine CC0 spritesheet. The three smooth landscape images are explicit negative controls, not claimed to have a recoverable pixel grid.
