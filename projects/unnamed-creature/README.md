# Unnamed Creature

Nobody knows what this thing is or why it is on a basketball. Move the pointer and its eye follows. Click or tap and it flinches as the scissors snip. Drag to look around.

Everything is modelled, textured and lit in code with three.js: no image textures and no downloaded models. Requires WebGL 2.

App modules are readable ES modules with no framework. `vendor/three-0.187.0/three-custom.min.js` is three.js tree-shaken to the exports this toy uses.
